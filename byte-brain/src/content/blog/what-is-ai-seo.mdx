The way people find information is changing. Instead of typing keywords into Google, many users now ask direct questions to AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity — and expect a single, complete answer.

This creates a new discipline: **AI SEO**, optimization for AI engines.

## What is AI SEO?

AI SEO is the set of practices that makes your content understood, cited and recommended by language models. If a customer asks "what's the best SEO company in Portugal?", AI SEO works so the answer includes you.

## How to optimize for AI engines

AI models value clear, structured content with context. In practice:

- **Answer questions directly.** Use FAQ-style sections with the question as the heading and a clear answer right after.
- **Use natural language.** Write the way people speak, not the way they fill a search box.
- **Structure with lists and short paragraphs.** This makes it easy for models to extract information.
- **Define entities and context.** Clearly state who you are, what you do and for whom.
- **Add structured data.** Schema.org helps machines interpret the content.

## Why it matters now

Most companies don't yet optimize for AI. That means there's a window of opportunity for first movers — just like the early days of traditional SEO.

Whoever builds the authority models recognize today will be present in tomorrow's answers, when this channel becomes dominant.
